Term Colors and Icons

Each term has its own color and seasonal icon, set in data/terms.yaml and updated once a year. They color the term bars on the classes page calendar, the class cards for each term, and the date badge, label, links and buttons of every event in that term. Events outside any term use Dodger Blue. The term colors are separate from the brand palette; they still need to meet WCAG AA contrast with white, since white text sits on them and they are used for small text on white.
