Sprouts

A joyful first step!

Ages 3–7

Schedule, locations and when to join ↓

This 50-minute weekly class blends rhythm, song, and playful movement to build the coordination, listening, and focus skills that form the foundation for violin study. Young children sing, tap, move, and explore music through scarves, rhythm instruments, and song stories in a warm, structured setting.

Designed for ages 3–7, the class develops timing, motor control, and musical awareness through familiar songs like Frog in the Meadow and Lightly Row, as well as classical favorites such as Fingal’s Cave and selections from standard Suzuki repertoire. Each session follows a consistent format that balances predictability with discovery - helping children feel confident, attentive, and excited about learning.

Tuition is $150 per term, with $50 due at registration and the remainder due 1 week before class starts.

Peninsula Community Sound Sprouts brings real music—classical, folk, and traditional songs—into children’s lives through play, movement, and family connection. Designed for children from infancy through early childhood, Sound Sprouts helps develop listening, rhythm, coordination, and connection with others while nurturing a lifelong appreciation for great music.

Classes combine singing, rhythm instruments, storytelling, and imaginative movement to masterpieces by Bach, Handel, and other composers. Every activity engages the whole child - body, mind, and spirit - through active listening, singing in tune, and keeping a steady beat.

Parents participate alongside their children, gaining simple tools to make music part of daily life. No musical background is required - just a willingness to sing, move, and connect.

Peninsula Community Sound Sprouts is not entertainment; it’s education made joyful - an invitation to grow together through real music, naturally.

Tuition is $150 per term, with $50 due at registration and the remainder due 1 week before class starts.

FAQ

  • What's the difference between Suzuki Sprouts and Sound Sprouts? The two classes have a lot in common. In both, parents are an important part of every class, and families sing, move, play rhythm instruments, and listen to great music together. The main difference is the violin: Sound Sprouts (ages 0–6) is all about music and movement, with no instrument needed, while Suzuki Sprouts (ages 3–7) also gently introduces violin study in a way that's right for young children.
  • Will my Suzuki Sprout be handed a violin at their first class? No. Most students will begin on a provided box violin and mock bow. Over the first term, they will learn violin skills, including how to handle the instrument and basic technique. When the parent and teacher agree they are ready, they may borrow a violin from our instrument library, or rent or purchase one elsewhere.
  • My child is 6 or 7. Should they join Suzuki Sprouts or a 6+ Beginner class? Suzuki Sprouts (ages 3–7) and our 6+ Beginner classes overlap on purpose, so each child can join the group that fits them best, regardless of age. Suzuki Sprouts teaches violin by ear through songs, games, and movement, and is a great fit for children who are not yet reading or who do best with short, active activities. 6+ Beginner classes teach violin, viola, cello, or bass and start reading music right away, so they suit children who can read and are ready to focus for about 30–45 minutes. Not sure which is right for your child? Email registrar@sequimcommunityorchestra.org and we'll help you decide.
