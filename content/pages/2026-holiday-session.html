This year's Holiday Session runs November 30 to December 18, 2026. Each group meets for three classes or rehearsals on a condensed version of its regular schedule. Sound Sprouts meets in Port Angeles; every other group meets in Sequim, and Port Angeles students are welcome to join them there. Sound Sprouts is open to everyone. The other groups are for students who already have the skills for their group, whether from our classes, school orchestra or lessons; they are not open to brand-new beginners.

Holiday Party

Friday, Dec. 18th at 6pm at St. Andrew's Episcopal Church

Join us to celebrate the season with music from our students. 510 E Park Ave, Port Angeles.

Tuition

Early registration (Oct. 15 to Oct. 31): $50
Late registration (after Oct. 31): $75

Register by November 1: if enough students sign up, we'll bring in section coaches from our local private teachers.

Questions? Email registrar@sequimcommunityorchestra.org.
